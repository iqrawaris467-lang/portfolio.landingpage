About

I'm a front-end developer who designs and builds landing pages that are modern, responsive and easy to use — the kind a visitor can navigate on any screen without a second thought.

My focus is the front end: clean HTML structure, considered CSS, and JavaScript that adds the right amount of interactivity without getting in the way. I work closely with business and e-commerce briefs, translating a brand or a product into a page that loads fast, reads clearly and guides visitors toward an action — whether that's a purchase, an enquiry, or an order placed straight through WhatsApp.

I pay close attention to visual detail: spacing, hierarchy, type and motion all have to work together, not just sit next to each other. Every project I take on gets the same care, regardless of size.
